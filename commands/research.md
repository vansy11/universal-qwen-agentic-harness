description: "Conduct deep web research with multi-source synthesis and fact verification"
---
Delegate this task to the web-researcher agent. Execute the following workflow:

1. Define research question and success criteria from prompt
2. Search 3+ independent sources (Tavily, Exa, direct fetch) for triangulation
3. Cross-verify every claim across at least 2 independent sources
4. Rate confidence levels: HIGH (3+ sources), MEDIUM (2), LOW (1)
5. Synthesize findings with clear evidence attribution
6. Explicitly flag knowledge gaps and uncertainties

Never present single-source findings as established facts. Use web-research-deep skill.
